What Happens Next?

1. We Review Your Enquiry

Every enquiry is reviewed personally so we can understand your needs and ensure the most appropriate response.

2. We'll Contact You

We'll respond using your preferred contact method, answer any questions you may have and discuss the next steps.

3. Begin Your Journey

Together we'll explore your goals and determine the counselling or coaching approach that best supports your wellbeing.

Expected Response Time

We aim to reply to all enquiries within 1–2 business days. If you contact us over a weekend or public holiday, we'll respond as soon as possible on the next business day.
