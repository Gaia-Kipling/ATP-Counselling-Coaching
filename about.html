Meet Your Counsellor

My Story

Counselling is more than a profession to me—it is a lifelong calling built on compassion, purpose and a genuine desire to help others discover their own strength.

I have always felt a deep calling to work with people. From my teenage years, I found myself drawn to understanding others, listening to their stories and helping them recognise their strengths and potential. That passion has grown into a fulfilling career as both a Registered Counsellor and an NLP Life Coach, allowing me the privilege of walking alongside individuals as they navigate healing, growth and personal transformation.

I am proudly registered with the Health Professions Council of South Africa (HPCSA) as a Registered Counsellor and have further expanded my professional skills through my NLP Life Coaching qualification with Action Factory Global. I believe learning never stops, which is why I continually invest in professional development, ongoing research and additional training to provide the highest standard of compassionate, evidence-informed support.

My passion lies in supporting individuals from all walks of life, regardless of their age, culture, background, beliefs or personal circumstances. I believe every individual has incredible resilience and untapped potential, even during life's most challenging seasons. Our sessions begin by understanding your unique experiences, but together we focus on building a meaningful path forward. Counselling is a collaborative journey, and I consider us a team working toward your goals.

A central part of my work is helping clients reconnect with their own strengths, values and purpose. I encourage people to recognise not only where they have been, but also who they are becoming. By exploring the purpose behind past experiences and discovering opportunities for future growth, we can transform obstacles into stepping stones toward a more fulfilling life.

Looking ahead, I am excited to expand my practice into Equine Assisted Psychotherapy (EAP). Combining my passion for mental health with the remarkable therapeutic benefits of horses will allow me to offer an additional, unique avenue of healing and personal development for future clients.

Above all, I believe that you are capable, resilient and worthy of living a life filled with purpose. You do not have to face life's challenges alone. Together we can build confidence, discover practical solutions and create lasting positive change—because you only live once, and every person deserves the opportunity to live with purpose.
